What three files can't show

  • Supplier cost changes, and how fast you passed them on to customers.
  • Vendor rebates and special pricing you earned but may not have claimed.
  • Quotes, so we can't tell a discount that won the order from one that didn't.
  • Customer contract prices outside the standard matrix.
  • Cost to serve each customer, such as delivery frequency and small-order handling.
  • Whether the yellow items were justified. Only your team can say.

How we read the files, and the sample data
  • The invoice file carries the system matrix price for each line. A line counts as below matrix when it is more than 2% under it.
  • Policy used: orders of $2,500 or more ship free. Expedited shipments and accessorials are always billable.
  • Credits are rated by type. Pricing errors, wrong items, duplicates and damaged freight with no carrier claim are red.
  • The 90-day total is not scaled to a year except where labeled as an estimate.
