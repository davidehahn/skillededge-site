Questions

Common questions

What do you need from me?
Two files by email: your overdue-invoice (aging) report and your maintenance-agreement renewal list. CSV or PDF is fine. I never need a login to your software.
Why not just use the reminders in my accounting software?
Built-in reminders are generic, and many shops never turn them on. This gives you a ranked list of who to chase first and messages that mention the job and the amount, so they read like you wrote them.
What if I have more than 15 overdue accounts?
The sprint covers the top 15 by dollars, where most of the money usually sits. You also get a simple reminder template for the rest.
What happens to my files?
They are used only to prepare your deliverables and are deleted 30 days after delivery.
Do I have to hand over customer information I am not comfortable sharing?
You can blank out customer names and send only invoice numbers, amounts and dates. The emails then use placeholders you fill in before sending.
