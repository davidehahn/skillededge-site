Where distributors lose money

The ERP records the transaction. It does not catch what people do around it.

Margin leaks in the gaps between systems and people. These are the places we look first.

Pricing overrides

Discounts and manual price changes that drift below target margin with no review.

Vendor cost changes

Supplier increases that reach your cost file faster than they reach your sell prices.

Freight and landed cost

Shipping and handling costs that are not fully recovered on the invoice.

Rebates and special pricing

Vendor program thresholds missed or claims never filed.

Customer profitability

High-volume accounts that cost more to serve than they return.

Repetitive office work

Quote follow-up, order status, AR chasing and reporting that consume skilled hours.

How the day works

No one pays to watch us experiment.

The work is done before the session. The day is for adapting it to your business and testing it on your real data.

  • Short intake: the process, who owns it, the systems involved, and how often it runs.
  • You select three workflows. We approve one and build and test it in advance.
  • On the day, we adapt it to your business and run your actual or sanitized files through it, including edge cases.
  • We measure before and after: time per run, frequency, errors and rework.
  • One group follow-up session after the cohort to review results and next steps.

Questions

Before you ask

Do you need access to our ERP?

Not for the Action Lab or the Margin Exposure Screen. The audit uses ERP transaction exports, and only after an NDA and a data-handling agreement.

Is this AI training?

No. The goal is a working, tested workflow on your own files and a measured result, not a class on AI tools.

Do you guarantee savings?

No. We measure and report time and capacity recovered, and flag margin exposure for investigation. Any dollar figures come from your own data.

Who should attend?

The person who owns the process we are fixing, usually an operations, finance, purchasing or sales-operations lead. One attendee per company.
