The QA Layer

The checks you'd never think to ask AI for.

Anyone can paste notes into a chatbot. What they get back sounds generic, invents citations and skims the attachments. The QA Layer is what makes the output safe to sign.

  • 01

    Data QA

    Every count, date and number checked against your notes. Twelve findings in the summary means twelve in the body. Summary, findings and corrective actions all agree.

  • 02

    Voice check

    Reads like you, not like a consultant template or a chatbot. Built from your own writing, with filler and AI phrasing stripped out.

  • 03

    Attachment QA

    Every hazard or item pulled into a table and counted. Open items, closures without evidence and anything the report never mentions get flagged.

  • 04

    Citation & revision check

    Every cited standard flagged for you to confirm. Referenced documents checked for current revision and dates in the right order.

  • 05

    Open-items rollup

    Every open item across the report and its attachments in one list, so nothing falls between sections.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Will the reports sound like AI wrote them?

No. The engine is built from your own past reports and tuned to your wording, sentence length and terminology. The voice check strips consultant filler and common AI phrasing. If it doesn't sound like you, we tune it until it does.

Does this replace my professional judgment?

No. The engine drafts and flags. You decide. Every finding, risk rating and certification statement is yours to review and sign. Risk ratings come from your own matrix, never from the AI.

What about client confidentiality?

We sign an NDA before you share anything. Client names and identifying details are removed from example reports before they're used. The engine is set up in your own AI account, so your live client work stays with you. Check your client contracts for any restrictions on AI tools, and we'll build around them.

Which AI tool does it run on?

The one you already use, on a paid plan, such as ChatGPT or Claude. If you don't have one yet, we'll recommend a plan during intake. No new software to learn.

Do I have to tell my clients I use AI?

Some agencies and contracts now require disclosure. We cover this during intake so the way you use the engine fits your client requirements.

My reports don't follow a standard template. Will this work?

Yes. That's why we start from your past reports, not a generic template. The shells give us a head start; your examples set the format.

Does the attachment check guarantee nothing is missed?

No tool can promise that, and we won't. It extracts and counts every item, reconciles against the document's own totals, and flags what needs your attention. It catches what's easy to overlook. You make the final call.
